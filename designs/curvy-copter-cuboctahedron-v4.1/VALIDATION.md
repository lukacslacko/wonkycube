# Validation scope

The [summary](reports/summary.json), [package audit](reports/package-audit.json) and [rounding regression](reports/rounding-regression.json) passed for the supplied v4.1 files. The publication preserves all delivered STL, 3MF, SVG, DXF and DWG bytes, with hashes in `publication.json` and `SHA256SUMS`.

- Closed, connected printable meshes; manifest hashes; geometry-only plates within a 256 mm bed.
- Both halves of the repaired R2 radial edge fillets on centers and floating corners; exact pocket contours retained.
- Spherical holding shoulders, 74 foot-only capture probes, and retention probes in nine solved, intermediate and jumbled configurations.
- All twelve half turns sampled every 3 degrees; five jumbling moves at 49 samples per move; a 48-step legal jumbling walk. Exact 0.5 mm inlay envelopes are included.
- Pocket backing, plug keying, insertion and removal, screw access, and layer connectivity at 0.16 and 0.20 mm.
- Fourteen exact face sheets with 104 closed outlines, the optional easy-fit sheets and test outlines; all 30 SVG/DXF/DWG sets present. Genuine DWGs were independently decoded and compared with their source contours.

The underlying spherical-retention v3.2 puzzle was [physically printed and assembled successfully](../curvy-copter-cuboctahedron-v3.2/physical-feedback.json). That is separate from the **unprinted v4.1 pockets, keyed plugs and corrected edge fillets**. No physical inlay/cutter fit, friction, force, wear or fatigue result is claimed for v4.1. Shell assembly inherits the prior mechanism's assembly evidence; the new plugs have a separately checked outside insertion path. See the reports for finite sampling and tolerances.
